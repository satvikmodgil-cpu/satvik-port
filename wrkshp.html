A Note on Equipment

You do not need massive prime lenses or high-end professional camera bodies to learn the art of wildlife photography. Impactful imagery comes from knowing your gear thresholds inside out, patient framing, and accurate timing. We will focus entirely on getting the sharpest, cleanest frames possible from whatever setup you currently own.
