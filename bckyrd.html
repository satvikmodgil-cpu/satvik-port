Making people aware of the importance of nature and wildlife

While documenting birds at a village pond for Backyardd Wilderness, I unexpectedly drew a large crowd of curious locals because of my photography gear. What started as a simple photography session soon turned into a meaningful conversation about nature and wildlife. I spoke to them about the difference between migratory and non-migratory birds, the importance of keeping ponds clean for these species, and how even small village water bodies become vital habitats during migration seasons. Seeing people — especially children — become fascinated by the beauty and behavior of birds reminded me that awareness is the first step toward conservation. Through Backyardd Wilderness, my goal is not only to capture wildlife through a lens, but also to inspire people to observe, respect, and protect the natural world around them.
